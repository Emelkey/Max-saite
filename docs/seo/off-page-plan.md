# MAX SITE off-page authority plan

This plan requires manual outreach and owner approval. It must not be automated into spam.

## Principles

1. Prefer relevant editorial mentions and partner case pages over bulk directory packages.
2. Use the branded anchor `MAX SITE`; do not force keyword-rich anchors.
3. Ask clients for a credit only with consent. If the link is compensated, use the appropriate relationship attribute.
4. Never create fake offices or Google Business Profiles in Wave 1 cities.
5. Record source URL, contact, status, agreement, publication date and target case in an outreach log.

## 90-day cadence

- 3–5 relevant referring domains per month as a quality target, not a guaranteed result.
- 1–2 partner or case mentions per month.
- 1 expert contribution or guest material per month.
- 1 useful asset update per month.

## Priority assets

- website cost estimator with transparent assumptions;
- launch QA checklist;
- editable website brief;
- decision guide for site types;
- annual Ukraine website-cost research with methodology;
- city-page SERP Decision Gate guide;
- technical-specification template.

## Manual profile review

Evaluate Clutch, DesignRush, IT-Rating, relevant Ukrainian business/digital catalogues, founder profiles, GitHub and partner pages. Publish only accurate business details and keep one consistent brand name.

## Prohibited tactics

- purchased packages of hundreds of links;
- sitewide keyword-rich client footer credits;
- automated guest-post networks;
- irrelevant international directories;
- fake reviews, awards, locations or team profiles.
