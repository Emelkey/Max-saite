# MAX SITE content-hub plan

Updated: 2026-08-28

## Editorial model

- Every article answers the decision question in the first screen.
- One article owns one informational intent in `seo/keyword-map.csv`.
- Every article links to one primary commercial page and 2–4 contextual resources.
- The author role, publication date, updated date and source list are visible.
- Factual claims use primary sources; business judgments are labelled as MAX SITE practice.
- No article is created only by replacing a city name.
- Review every 6–12 months, and update the visible date only after substantive changes.
